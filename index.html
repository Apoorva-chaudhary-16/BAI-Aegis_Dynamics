The Problem

India's cities are undergoing rapid urbanization. This growth puts immense pressure on public infrastructure, making it challenging to ensure road safety and provide rapid response to incidents like accidents, breakdowns, or public disturbances. Police can not be present everywhere everytime, and in critical moments every second counts!

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: A future where every citizen in India's bustling urban centers feels secure on every street, at any time of day.

Mission: To deploy an immediate, intelligent, and autonomous response network for public safety incidents, creating a proactive shield of security for modern Indian cities.
